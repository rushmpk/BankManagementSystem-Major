Online Mobile Recharge

Now you no longer need to rush to the vendor for buying prepaid mobile recharge codes every time your talk time runs out. Just top-up your prepaid mobile cards by logging into Internet Banking on mpkBank.com. What's more, this online mobile recharge service is absolutely free for all MPK Bank Account holders.

Steps for Prepaid Mobile Recharge

1. Please login to www.mpkbank.com
2. Go to Payments & Transfer, select Prepaid Recharge.
3. Click on Prepaid Mobile Recharge.
4. Select your operator name, enter your mobile number and amount of recharge.
5. Enter One Time Password (OTP) to complete your transaction. Your Prepaid Mobile Recharge request will be processed in a few minutes.
